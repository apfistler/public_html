Parties & Events

Add a little mystery, laughter, and self-discovery to your next gathering.

Looking for something a little different for your next party, celebration, or social gathering?

I can bring handwriting analysis and astrology to your event and give your guests an entertaining, interactive experience they'll be talking about long after the party is over.

Something Different for Your Guests

Imagine handing me a sample of your handwriting and hearing me describe things about your personality, habits, relationships, motivations, and the way you approach life.

Then imagine your friends standing around listening, laughing, comparing notes, and saying: "That's totally you!"

That's the fun of bringing handwriting analysis and astrology to a party.

What Can I Do at Your Event?

Handwriting Analysis

Guests provide a handwriting sample and receive a brief, entertaining look at characteristics and personality traits reflected in their writing.

It's fascinating, personal, and often surprisingly revealing.

Astrology & Birth Charts

Explore personality and life themes through the unique configuration of a person's birth chart.

Move beyond the usual "What's your sign?" conversation and discover what the rest of the chart has to say.

A Combination of Both

Why choose one?

Combining handwriting analysis and astrology gives guests two completely different perspectives to compare, discuss, and enjoy.

The Fun Is Watching Everyone React

The best part isn't simply hearing what I discover about one person.

It's watching everyone else react.

Friends recognize each other in the descriptions. Couples compare notes. Someone inevitably says, "How did you know that?"

And before long, everyone wants to know what their own handwriting or birth chart has to say.

What starts as a little entertainment can turn into fascinating conversations about personality, relationships, and the people we think we know best.

Why Add This to Your Event?

Interactive

Guests aren't just watching a performance. They're participating in it.

Personal

Everyone gets something different because everyone's handwriting and birth chart is unique.

Conversation Starter

Give your guests something unexpected to talk about and compare with one another.

Memorable

It's the kind of experience people remember because they weren't simply watching it happen — they were part of it.

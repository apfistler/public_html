Groups & Workshops

Learn, practice, and explore together.

Some changes are easier to make when we don't try to make them alone. Groups and workshops provide an opportunity to learn new ideas, practice useful skills, ask questions, and connect with other people who are working toward change.

Learning Together

Groups and workshops offer a different way to explore behavior change, recovery, mindfulness, and personal development.

Rather than simply reading about an idea, participants have an opportunity to discuss it, ask questions, try techniques, and consider how the material applies to their own lives.

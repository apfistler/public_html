The Two Types of Smokers!

There are two types of smokers, each requiring a different type of plans. The first is a Casual Smoker. These are the people who smoke only occasionally and have been smoking for less than ten years. More often than not, these individuals simply need help with the behavioral aspect of smoking and only require very short-term treatment. The second type of smoker is the Regular Smoker. They are the people who have been smoking for a long while and smoke on a daily basis. Generally speaking they are the smokers who become irritated, angry, stressed or frustrated when they can't smoke. Treatment for Regular Smokers may require approximately six sessions to see results. But by blending the old technology of direct suggestion with the newer modalities that first removes the reasons for the poor habit, I are now able to resolve the underlying issue in both situations with a success rate of close to 95%.

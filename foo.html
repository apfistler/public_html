About the Presenter: Adam Fistler

Adam Fistler is a Behavioral Change Consultant, Board Certified Hypnotist, Certified 5-PATH® Hypnotist, and former owner of the Baltimore Hypnosis Center. Certified by the National Guild of Hypnotists and member of the International 5-PATH® Hypnosis Association, Adam has studied hypnosis, behavior change, and the mind for over two decades.

His perspective is shaped by first-hand experience with addiction and recovery across South Jersey and Philadelphia. Combining professional expertise with lessons from his own journey, Adam delivers practical tools grounded in empathy, curiosity, and the belief that people are more than the behaviors they are trying to overcome.
